## What the story actually demands

The central failure in the comic is that individually reasonable answers interact badly across one life: a meeting steals the hour needed for research while an independently recommended rewrite consumes the backup evening (pages 1–8). The intended entity carries goals, tradeoffs, history, uncertainty, and unfinished commitments across these tasks. The later payoff is **selective, reason-sensitive revision**, not merely remembering a biography. [C, pp1–8][C]

The strongest compact example is pages 31–37. Alice warns about a new project; the host overrides her; the project succeeds technically but causes a missed research deadline. They then revise two different assumptions: Alice's workload estimate was better, while the host's estimate of the project's value was better. At the next opportunity she recalls only the valuable distinction: worthwhile opportunity, bad timing. A later relationship agreement permits a future challenge but requires evidence each time. An undifferentiated reward, a single success flag, or a global preference to reject new projects loses the behavior this story specifies. [C, lines459–576][C-mixed]

## Concrete dependency trace for the first slice

This is a requirements trace, not a replacement for the separate PE/MFM construction lanes:

1. Record two goals and their relevant constraints as scoped, versioned evidence/state. One opportunity competes for time with an existing commitment.
2. Assemble consumed evidence and personal-state versions; invoke PE for the native verdict and reasons before calling a feature generator.
3. Preserve the decision, prediction/assumptions, warning, owner override and authorized action. Completing an action is not proof of achieving its goal.
4. Record a mixed observed outcome with separate dimensions: technical/value benefit and time/deadline consequence. Preserve both rather than compressing to “success” or “failure.”
5. Let MFM propose the correction and outcome interpretation, with exact support. The gate accepts a scoped successor state or leaves it uncertain; it does not manufacture semantic correctness from a valid schema.
6. On a later matched opportunity, recollect relevant current state and earlier reasons. The native verdict should change timing or allocation while retaining the supported benefit. An irrelevant evidence change must leave the relevant judgment stable.
7. Trace the change through the accepted successor and the actual evidence consumed. Test a removed/contradicted/revoked successor and a matched baseline separately. Success here would support a narrow reason-sensitive personal-judgment claim; it would not establish lifelong companionship, complete self-improvement, all modalities, or automatic multi-user construction.

The independent variables should be the host correction, observed outcome component, and relevance of the updated state. The important mediator is the admitted state actually consumed by native judgment. Human-readable citations or a changed final sentence alone do not show that mediation. A mechanical fixture can validate plumbing, but learned claims require the real qualified component outputs.

## Required ownership and model boundary

The backbone must own durable evidence/claim transitions, references and versions, scope/permissions, mission and action state, outcome lineage, actual context consumption, recovery, and cross-representation correction/deletion. It must connect the semantic producers and consumers so that a new outcome can affect the right future decision. These responsibilities do not require one service or neural model per noun.

PE owns learned identity-conditioned native judgment within the evidence and authority envelope; its role is not general feature generation or canonical autobiographical storage. MFM interprets new experience and proposes semantic changes; it does not choose a physical database as truth authority. Host, relationship, self, world, causal, predictive, procedural and retrieval roles can use structured state, learned artifacts, or shared components as their own objective warrants. A deterministic gate validates eligibility and controlled transitions; passing it is not proof that every semantic proposal is factually correct. Feature engines supply replaceable capabilities after personal judgment and egress control. FBM constructs and improves the assembled system, but cannot promote itself on its own score or inherit Alice's private state. [A1 §§3–8; A2 D10–D11, Lane I; F1 §§Conversation, Builder][A1]

## Implementation evidence and specification gaps

- **Working foundation documented on main:** P5.1a has SQLite WAL storage of metadata-only ExperienceEvent envelopes, atomic writes, contiguous sequence numbers, scope binding and integrity checks. It explicitly does not implement learning curation or automatic retention. This is evidence of a persistent ledger foundation, not a learned feedback loop. No tests were rerun for this audit. [A11 lines5–40][A11]
- **Mission/workspace remain contract-only on main:** immutable IDs, successor validation, six routing actions, Result Capsules, traceback, attention and workspace projections exist. The milestone documents explicitly exclude persistent autonomous mission/attention runtimes and full UI. [A6 lines5–32; A8 lines5–51][A6]
- **Result linkage is necessary but incomplete evidence of outcome learning:** `ResultCapsule` has status (`succeeded`, `partial`, `failed`, `cancelled`, `blocked`), summary digest, outputs, evidence and source events. `TracebackChain` records propagation/reopen/follow-up/blocker/conflict operations. The record can point to rich outcome evidence, but the contract alone does not interpret which assumption was wrong, perform causal credit assignment, or update later judgment. A successor design needs that semantic path; it need not blindly enlarge this metadata envelope. [A7 lines23–65,155–177,186–202][A7]
- **Whole-life coordination is not already solved by the Mission Graph snapshot:** current `MissionGraphSnapshot` belongs to one mission, requires all nodes/edges in that mission, and validates a single-parent rooted parent-child tree. Other typed links exist, but cross-mission conflict/goal reasoning needs an explicit broader cognitive relation path or versioned extension. This is a contract scope observation, not a diagnosed runtime bug. [A12 lines56–65,508–518,595–638][A12]
- **Native conversation is destination work:** the personal-development architecture and FIRST_RELEASE explicitly say the local verdict/expression/generator-comparison loop is not established by the older Phase 3 Qwen adapter. Storage, schemas, a provider's agreement/disagreement, or fluent text cannot close it. [A4 lines135–184; F1 lines35–58][A4]
- **The comic is an intended behavior suite:** no source reviewed establishes forty-year continuity, autonomous science, complete deletion/unlearning, complete consumer build or learned outcome-sensitive judgment. Fable's current README acknowledges no released app and no demonstrated complete loop; broader implementation claims require current lane receipts and behavior tests. [F README lines91–151][F-readme]

## Contradictions and points to preserve explicitly

1. **Engine lists have drifted between repositories.** Fable's current v1 profile still selects JetStream as event fabric and JanusGraph for scale-out, calling Kurrent a challenger (lines95–117,145). ALICE main's September 27 execution map and identity architecture select Kurrent event authority, NATS transport, and NebulaGraph (A2 lines364–386,430–459; A1 lines281–298). FIRST_RELEASE says exact engines may change without shrinking logical capability. A new design should name one canonical choice or a deliberate hypothesis and migration boundary; silently mixing these tables is unsound. The present user request authorizes reconsideration. [F2][A2]
2. **“Encrypted API signals” is not an established private-computation mechanism.** Fable README language is qualified by FIRST_RELEASE: TLS/redaction/placeholders do not hide approved semantic content from the feature provider. Egress must disclose destination, purpose and permitted payload; provider outputs remain candidates. [F1 lines60–70][F1]
3. **“No amount of personal data is enough” is broader than an experimentally supportable comparison.** The same README narrows the actual stronger hypothesis to limited data, changing circumstances and consistent judgment. FloRA's positive result would support that scoped comparison and its tested conditions; it would not establish that all direct personalization fails or that substituting a frontier backbone automatically wins. [F README Main Hypothesis and FloRA §§2–3][F-readme]
4. **“Five starting roles” is not a topology constraint.** MFM/personality separation is explicit; other responsibilities need correct subject/objective/authority boundaries, not necessarily five neural artifacts. Do not add a model solely because a row in a table has a name, or omit a necessary learned controller because it would be a sixth model. [A1 §§3–4; F1 lines13–25][A1]
5. **Preserving source identity does not freeze the assistant.** Ordinary host data cannot rewrite Elaina's identity anchor; ALICE's own later self and relationship must still develop. A general Fable also cannot treat the host's ideal-self declaration as canonical fact. [A1 lines18–85; A4 lines12–27][A1]
6. **Scope is not destination.** Full Fable v1 includes the complete transferable personal foundation; FloRA may exercise one narrow connected causal slice. A small experiment must be honest about missing capabilities, while full-release requirements must not turn into prerequisites for testing the narrow hypothesis. The current source map's older N0 execution plan is historical lane context, not authority to restart that training program. [F1 lines84–111; F README FloRA question; A2 §0][F1]
7. **Permission is scoped, not perpetual per-step ceremony.** The comic includes explicit approval before sending and a later pre-authorized rule to halt bad instrument runs. Preserve meaningful host control, overrides, revocation and uncertainty; do not derive a universal confirmation before every action from one story panel. [C pp30,32,50][C-action]
